## 2. 📅 Comprehensive Appointment Booking Form
- **Customer Information**: Full Name, Mobile (+91), Email, City Selector, Famous Location / Campus Hub, and Emergency Alternate Contact.
- **Driver & Vehicle Information**: Driver Name, Driving License No. (DL No.), Vehicle Plate, and Vehicle Type (Sedan, SUV, EV, Motorcycle).
- **Service & Slot Scheduler**: Service selection, Date Picker, and Preferred Time Slot.
- **Instant Digital QR Pass**: Real-time bay assignment, QR Pass generation with vehicle plate, bay ID, security PIN, and city/campus location.
